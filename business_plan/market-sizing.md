| Monthly visits | 27M+ | SimilarWeb (May 2025) |

**Calculation:**
- If UAE+Saudi+Brazil+Mexico = 100,000 sellers
- UAE is most mature but smaller population
- Estimate UAE share: **15,000-25,000 sellers** (15-25%)

**Alternative calculation:**
- 27M monthly visits ÷ 260 customers/seller = **~104,000 potential customers**
- If 15% are repeat → ~15,600 unique customer accounts
- Industry average: 10-20 customers per active seller
- Estimate: **8,000-15,000 active Amazon UAE sellers**

## 9. Key Assumptions & Risks

### Assumptions

1. **Noon seller count:** 5,000 FBN sellers scales to 7,000-10,000 total
2. **Amazon UAE sellers:** Similar to Noon or slightly higher
3. **Multi-marketplace rate:** 20-25% of sellers use 2+ platforms
4. **Willingness to pay:** 50% of multi-MP sellers would pay for a solution
5. **Conversion rate:** 10-15% of leads become customers

### Risks to Estimates

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Actual seller count lower | TAM reduced | Focus on quality over quantity |
| Lower multi-MP rate | SAM reduced | Expand to single-MP sellers |
| Price sensitivity higher | ARPU reduced | Offer freemium tier |
| Competition enters | Share reduced | Move fast, build moat |
