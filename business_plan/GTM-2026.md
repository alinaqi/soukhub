# SoukHub Go-To-Market Plan 2026
## AI-Powered Multi-Marketplace Management for UAE Sellers

---

## Executive Summary

This GTM plan outlines a phased, gate-controlled approach to launching SoukHub in the UAE. Each phase has clear success metrics and go/no-go decision points.

**Timeline:** January - December 2026
**Market:** UAE (Amazon.ae, Noon, Cartlow, Revibe)
**Target:** Multi-marketplace e-commerce sellers
**Primary Acquisition:** Direct outreach + content marketing + partnerships
**Total Budget:** AED 350,000 - 500,000 (Year 1)

---

## Phase Overview

| Phase | Months | Focus | Critical Gate |
|-------|--------|-------|---------------|
| **0: Pre-Launch** | Jan | MVP + 20 seller leads | Working Amazon + Noon sync |
| **1: Proof of Concept** | Feb-Mar | 15 paying customers | 80% retention, NPS 40+ |
| **2: Early Traction** | Apr-Jun | 75 customers, prove unit economics | Positive contribution margin |
| **3: Growth** | Jul-Sep | 200 customers, add Cartlow/Revibe | 3x MoM growth |
| **4: Scale** | Oct-Dec | 400+ customers, Series A ready | AED 150K MRR |

- If Noon integration problematic, go Amazon-only
- Add Noon later

**Pivot C: Target Different Segment**
- If SMEs don't pay, target mid-market
- Raise prices, add features

## KEY ASSUMPTIONS

1. **Conversion Rate:** 20%+ of trials convert to paid
2. **ARPU:** AED 300-400/month average
3. **Churn:** <10% monthly after first 3 months
4. **CAC:** AED 800-1,500 per customer
5. **API Access:** Noon provides API access for integration
6. **Market Timing:** No major competitor launches Noon integration in 2026
