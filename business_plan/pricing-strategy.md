## Key Decisions

### Why Not Usage-Only Pricing?

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Scales with success | Unpredictable revenue |
| Low barrier | Customers fear surprise bills |
| Fair | Hard to forecast |

**Decision:** Tiered + overage hybrid gives predictability with upside

### Why Not Lower Prices?

- Signals low quality
- Attracts price-sensitive churners
- Hard to raise later
- Need margins for support

**Decision:** Price for value, discount strategically

### Why AED Not USD?

- Local currency = trust
- No FX confusion
- Aligns with customer bank accounts
- Shows UAE commitment
